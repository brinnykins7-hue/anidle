How to Play

  • Select anime series above, then hit Start Game.
  • Type a character name — autocomplete will help you pick.
  • After each guess, 7 colored cells reveal clues:
  • Green — exact match
  • Yellow — close (age/height ±15%; hair/eyes share a color)
  • Red — wrong
  • ↑ / ↓ on age & height show which direction to guess.
  • You have 20 attempts per game.
